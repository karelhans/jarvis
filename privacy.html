Jarvis — Privacy Policy

Jarvis is personal software operated by its owner for their own household. It is not offered to the public and has no third-party users.

  • Jarvis accesses only the operator's own Google account data (such as Gmail) through Google APIs, with the operator's explicit consent, solely to organize the operator's personal tasks and reminders.
  • All data is processed and stored on the operator's own devices. No data is sold, shared, or transmitted to third parties.
  • No data is collected from anyone other than the operator's own accounts.
  • Jarvis's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
